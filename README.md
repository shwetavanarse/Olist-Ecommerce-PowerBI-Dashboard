# 📌 Overview

The **Olist E-commerce Analytics Dashboard** is a comprehensive **Power BI Business Intelligence project** developed using the **Brazilian Olist E-commerce Dataset**.

The objective of this project is to provide stakeholders with a centralized reporting solution for monitoring:

- 📈 Sales Performance
- 📦 Product Performance
- 🌍 Regional Revenue
- 🚚 Delivery Efficiency
- 👥 Customer Activity
- ⭐ Customer Satisfaction

The dashboard leverages **Power Query**, **Data Modeling**, **DAX**, and **interactive visualizations** to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights.

# 🎯 Business Problem

Managing an e-commerce platform involves tracking thousands of orders, customers, products, sellers, and deliveries across multiple regions.

Without centralized reporting, it becomes difficult to:

- Monitor business performance
- Identify high-performing products
- Track delivery efficiency
- Analyze regional sales
- Measure customer satisfaction
- Support data-driven decision making

---

# 💡 Solution

This Power BI dashboard consolidates data from multiple sources into an interactive reporting solution that enables users to:

- Monitor KPIs in real time
- Analyze sales trends
- Explore product performance
- Compare regional revenue
- Evaluate delivery metrics
- Drill into business insights using interactive visualizations

# 💼 Business Insights

### 📈 Sales Performance

- Generated **$16.01M** in total revenue.
- Processed **96K orders**.
- Served approximately **99K customers**.

---

### 📦 Product Performance

- Beauty & Health and Watches & Gifts generate the highest revenue.
- Revenue is concentrated within a few top-performing product categories.

---

### 🌍 Regional Performance

- São Paulo contributes the highest revenue.
- Southern and southeastern states dominate overall sales.
- Revenue concentration highlights opportunities for regional expansion.

---

### 🚚 Delivery Performance

- Average delivery time is **12 days**.
- More than **93%** of deliveries are completed on time.
- Roraima, Amapá, and Amazonas experience the highest delivery times.

---

### ⭐ Customer Satisfaction

- Average customer review score is **4.09 / 5**.
- Overall customer experience remains positive despite regional delivery challenges.
